Privacy

BoxCoach · last updated October 2026

The short version

BoxCoach has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The camera is read on your phone, and everything the app knows about your training stays on your phone.

Camera

When you train with the camera on, BoxCoach finds your body's position in each frame on your phone to count punches and coach your form. Video and photos are not recorded, stored or uploaded by the app.

Your training data

Sessions, notes, check-ins, settings and the punches you teach it are saved only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them. You can export a backup file yourself in Settings; where you keep it is up to you.

Things you choose to share

  • Reports and frame photos ("Copy report", "Report a problem", "Send to Claude") go only where you send them from your phone's share sheet.
  • Optional Claude punch check: if you add your own Anthropic API key in Settings and switch it on, cropped frames of a video you analyse are sent to Anthropic to check the punches. It's off unless you turn it on, and your key stays on your phone.

Downloads

The website version downloads the pose-tracking model the first time you use the camera. The App Store and Play Store versions include it, so they download nothing.

Children

BoxCoach isn't aimed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use Coach → Settings → Report a problem in the app, or see Help.
