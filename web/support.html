BoxCoach support

Report a problem

In the app: Coach → Settings → Report a problem. Say what happened; the app adds its version, your phone model and the last errors it hit, and you choose where to send it. No video is included.

It miscounts or misreads my punches

  • Phone at chest height, 2–3 m away, whole body in view, light in front of you.
  • Run the punch test (Train → Punch test). It calls 10 of each punch, shows what it got wrong, and learns how your punches look from that camera spot.
  • Moved the phone somewhere new? Run the test again from there.

The camera won't start

Allow camera access for BoxCoach in your phone's settings, then reopen the app. The first camera session on the website needs an internet connection to download the tracking model.

Keeping your sessions safe

Your training data lives only on your phone. Make a backup in Coach → Settings → Export backup now and then, and keep the file somewhere safe. Deleting the app deletes the data.
